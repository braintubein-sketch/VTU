Mastering the VTU 2022 Scheme

By Braintube Editorial January 28, 2026 8 min read

The Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) introduced the 2022 Scheme with several significant changes aimed at aligning engineering education with modern industry requirements. Whether you are a first-year student just starting your journey or a senior transitioning through the semesters, understanding the nuances of this scheme is crucial for academic success.

Understanding the Credit System

One of the most important aspects of the 2022 scheme is the credit-based system. Each subject is assigned a specific number of credits based on its importance and the number of contact hours per week. Typically, core subjects carry 4 credits, while electives and labs carry fewer. To graduate, a student must accumulate a total of around 160 credits over four years.

CIE vs. SEE: The Balancing Act

The evaluation is divided into Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and Semester End Examination (SEE). In the 2022 scheme, the weightage is often 50:50. This means your performance in internal tests, assignments, and mini-projects is just as important as the final exam.

  • CIE (50 Marks): Scaled from three internal tests, assignments, and quizzes.
  • SEE (50 Marks): The final theory exam conducted by VTU.

Tips for Excelling in the 2022 Scheme

To stay ahead, you need a structured approach to your studies. Here are some proven strategies:

  1. Focus on Internal Tests: Since CIE accounts for 50% of your grade, never take internal tests lightly. Scoring high in CIE reduces the pressure during SEE.
  2. Utilize the Syllabus Fully: VTU provides detailed syllabus copies. Use them as a checklist to ensure you haven't missed any topics.
  3. Practice Previous Years' Papers: Even with a new scheme, several topics repeat. Practicing papers can help you understand the question pattern.

Conclusion

The VTU 2022 scheme is designed to be student-friendly but requires consistent effort. By understanding the credit system and focusing on both CIE and SEE, you can achieve a stellar CGPA. Stay tuned to Braintube for more subject-specific guides and notes!
