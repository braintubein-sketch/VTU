How to Score 9.0+ CGPA in VTU Exams

By Braintube Editorial January 29, 2026 10 min read

Scoring a high CGPA in VTU is often perceived as a daunting task. However, with the right combination of smart work, consistent preparation, and effective exam-writing techniques, reaching that elusive 9.0+ mark is entirely possible. In this guide, we break down the exact strategy used by toppers to dominate their semester exams.

1. Master the Blueprint

Every VTU exam follows a specific pattern (The Question Paper Blueprint). You usually have 5 modules, and you must answer one full question from each module. Strategy: Identify which modules are easiest for you and master them first. However, to cross 90, you must be proficient in all 5 modules. Don't skip any module entirely!

2. Presentation is King

VTU evaluators often have hundreds of papers to correct. A neat, well-structured paper stands out.

  • Use Diagrams: Even if not asked, draw a diagram if the concept allows it. Use a pencil and ruler.
  • Bullet Points: Avoid long paragraphs. Use bullet points for easy scanning.
  • Highlight Keywords: Underline important definitions or terms.

3. The "Test-2-Test" Consistency

Don't wait for the preparatory exams to start studying. The internal tests (CIE) are your best friends. If you score 45-50 out of 50 in CIE, you only need a modest score in SEE to get an 'S' grade (10 grade points).

4. Effective Note-Taking

Don't just rely on textbooks. Textbooks are often too detailed for exam preparation. Use curated notes (like the ones on Braintube) that focus on specific, exam-oriented questions. Creating your own summary sheets for formulas and definitions during the semester is a game-changer.

5. Solve Previous 3 Question Papers

About 60-70% of VTU questions are based on repeated patterns. Solving at least the last 3 question papers gives you a huge advantage and boosts your confidence immensely.

Final Word

Consistency beats intensity. Studying for 2 hours every day is far more effective than studying 15 hours a day for one week before exams. Discipline your routine, use quality resources, and you'll see your CGPA soar.
