VTU 2022 Scheme Grading System

Grade Description Marks Range Grade Points
O Outstanding 90 - 100 10
A+ Excellent 80 - 89 9
A Very Good 70 - 79 8
B+ Good 60 - 69 7
B Above Average 55 - 59 6
C Average 50 - 54 5
P Pass 40 - 49 4
F Fail 0 - 39 0
CGPA to Percentage Conversion:
Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

📌 Important Notes:

  • SGPA is calculated for individual semesters
  • CGPA is the weighted average of all SGPAs
  • Failed subjects (F grade) are included with 0 grade points
  • Minimum CGPA required for degree: 4.00
  • Credits typically range from 20-26 per semester
