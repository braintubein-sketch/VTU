Our Mission

Braintube is Karnataka's premier student-centric academic platform, specifically engineered for the VTU 2022 & 2025 Scheme. We serve as a bridge between complex university syllabi and student success by providing structured, peer-reviewed educational resources powered by AI.

Unlike generic resource portals, every set of notes and study material on Braintube undergoes rigorous quality reviews. Our content is curated by top-performing students and academic contributors who understand the specific nuances of VTU examination patterns.

By leveraging technology and collective academic intelligence, we democratize high-quality engineering education across all VTU-affiliated colleges.
